An agent that remembers everything will eventually run out of money. An agent that forgets at random becomes useless. Memento treats memory as a portfolio of future usefulness: keep what cannot be replaced, condense what is useful, and turn down the noise.

The decision is the product, so every decision is open to inspection. budgetGate in src/lib/agent/gate.ts compares integer base units and returns one sentence. That exact sentence is what shows up in the UI, in the receipt, and in the API response. There is nowhere that a refusal gets narrated instead of computed.

You can make it decide right now without a wallet or a signup. Drag the monthly spending cap and press "Run a live decision now". Memento reads this wallet's Filecoin Pay balance, runway, payment rails, and the onchain price list at the current epoch, then runs the real policy engine and budget gate. Set the cap below the projected cost and the same funded account gets refused, with a four step trace showing what it read, what it selected, what it priced, and which limit stopped it.
