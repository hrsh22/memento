An agent that keeps everything runs out of money. An agent that forgets at random is useless. Memento sits in between: hang on to what you cannot get back, shorten what is still useful, and let the noise go.

You can make it decide right now, no wallet or signup. Drag the spending cap and it reads this wallet's real Filecoin Pay balance and runway at the current epoch before deciding. Set the cap under what storage costs and a funded account turns down its own write, and tells you why.
